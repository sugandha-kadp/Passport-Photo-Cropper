Australian passport photo rules to check

  • 35–40 mm wide by 45–50 mm high. This tool uses 35 × 45 mm.
  • Face from chin to crown 32–36 mm, centred and looking straight at the camera.
  • Plain white or light grey background, even lighting, no shadows.
  • Neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open. No glasses.

Supported image types

  • All modern browsers: JPG / JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF (first frame), BMP, AVIF.
  • HEIC / HEIF (iPhone photos): open directly in Safari. In Chrome, Edge and Firefox the tool converts them first, which needs an internet connection and takes a few seconds.
  • TIFF: Safari only. In other browsers, save it as JPG or PNG first.
  • Exports: JPG at maximum quality, or lossless PNG.
